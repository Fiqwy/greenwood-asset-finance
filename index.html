    Good to know

    Questions, answered

    What does it cost to use Greenwood Asset Finance?

    In most asset finance deals the lender pays our commission, so our service usually comes at no direct cost to you. If a fee ever applies to your situation, we tell you up front and in writing before you commit.

    What can you finance?

    Cars and utes, trucks and trailers, earthmoving and plant, boats, bikes, caravans, business equipment and more. If it helps your business earn or your family get moving, talk to us and we will tell you honestly whether we can help.

    I am a new business or sole trader. Can you still help?

    Often, yes. Different lenders have different appetites, and part of our job is knowing which ones suit newer ABNs and self employed applicants. Get in touch and we will talk it through.

    Will an enquiry affect my credit score?

    Making an enquiry with us does not affect your credit. We only move to a formal lender application, which can involve a credit check, once you understand your options and give us the go ahead.

    How is an estimate different from a quote?

    The estimator on this page is a simple calculation on numbers you choose. A real quote reflects an actual lender, your circumstances and the asset, including fees and the rate on offer. We will get you a real quote when you are ready.

    What do you need to get started?

    To begin, just your name, the best way to reach you, and a rough idea of what you are financing. We will guide you through anything else from there.
