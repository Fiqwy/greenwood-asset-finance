What we arrange

Finance built around the asset

From a single vehicle to the equipment that runs your operation, we structure finance to suit how your business actually works.

Chattel mortgage

You own the asset from day one while the lender holds security over it. A popular structure for GST registered businesses managing cash flow and depreciation.

Commercial hire purchase

The lender buys the asset and your business hires it across the term, taking full ownership once the final payment is made.

Finance lease

Use the asset for an agreed term with a residual at the end. A flexible way to access equipment without owning it outright.

Motor vehicle & fleet

Finance for utes, cars, trucks and trailers, from a single purchase to a growing fleet, structured around how the vehicles are used.

Equipment & machinery

Funding for the plant, machinery and gear your business runs on, arranged so you can acquire what you need without tying up working capital.

Working capital

Business funding to smooth cash flow, fund growth or bridge timing gaps, arranged to suit how your revenue actually comes in.

Not sure which structure suits you? Talk it through with us

Good to know

Questions, answered

What does it cost to use Greenwood?

In most asset finance deals the lender pays our commission, so our service usually comes at no direct cost to you. If a fee ever applies to your situation, we tell you up front and in writing before you commit.

What can you finance?

Vehicles, trucks and trailers, plant and machinery, and a wide range of business equipment. If it helps your business earn, talk to us and we will tell you honestly whether we can help.

I am a new business or sole trader. Can you still help?

Often, yes. Different lenders have different appetites, and part of our job is knowing which ones suit newer ABNs and self employed applicants. Get in touch and we will talk it through.

Will an enquiry affect my credit score?

Making an enquiry with us does not affect your credit. We only move to a formal lender application, which can involve a credit check, once you understand your options and give us the go ahead.

How is an estimate different from a quote?

The estimator on this page is a simple calculation on numbers you choose. A real quote reflects an actual lender, your circumstances and the asset, including fees and the rate on offer. We will get you a real quote when you are ready.

What do you need to get started?

To begin, just your name, the best number to reach you, and a rough idea of what you are financing. We will guide you through anything else from there.
